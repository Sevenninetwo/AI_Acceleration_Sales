Prospecting
Reps can't get enough high-quality leads to fill the top of funnel
Lead Generation Engine
  • Apollo.io filters leads by ICP criteria (industry, headcount, tech stack, funding stage)
  • Clay enriches each contact with technographics, hiring signals, and funding rounds, then auto-scores them
  • Claude writes hyper-personalised outreach for the top-scored leads
  • Reps only touch leads that are already warm and enriched

  • Counts frequency and drafts a monthly escalation brief for corporate - data, not anecdotes
