# Sales AI Acceleration Playbook

A practical reference of AI-powered workflows sorted by sales stage - from prospecting through pipeline management and leadership operations.

## What This Is

An interactive table mapping 9 sales workflows to specific AI solutions, tool stacks, and success metrics. Each workflow is categorised by sales stage (Prospecting, Account Planning, Discovery, Proposal, Pipeline Management, Leadership & Ops) and can be filtered in the live page.

👉 [View the live playbook](https://your-subdomain.netlify.app)

## Workflows Covered

- **Prospecting** - ICP-filtered lead generation (Apollo + Clay + Claude), weekly trigger signal digest
- **Account Planning** - Whitespace mapping across product portfolio, pre-meeting brief generation
- **Discovery** - Deal architecture prompts for multi-thread opportunity mapping
- **Proposal & Pitch** - Platform narrative generator to move reps from point-product to platform selling
- **Pipeline Management** - Daily next-best-action briefs, stalled deal analysis with unblock recommendations
- **Leadership & Ops** - Blocker logging with automated categorisation and escalation briefs

## Tool Stack Architecture

Each workflow uses a consistent four-layer stack:

| Layer | Role | Examples |
|-------|------|----------|
| **Brain** | AI reasoning | Claude API, Clay AI, Perplexity |
| **Interface** | Where output lands | Slack, Google Sheets, Notion |
| **Connector** | Automation glue | Zapier |
| **Data** | Input source | Salesforce exports, CRM notes, account lists |
